# 01 — Product brief

## One line
Pick a Gemini image or video → get the same file back without the visible sparkle logo.
No signup, no ads in v1, works on web, Android and iPhone.

## Who it is for
People who make images and short videos with Gemini and want a clean version for a post,
a slide or a thumbnail. Mostly on phones. Many will arrive from a search like
"remove gemini watermark".

## Jobs to be done
1. "I have a Gemini image on my phone, make it clean in one tap."
2. "I have many images, clean them all at once."
3. "I have a Veo video, clean it and let me save it to my gallery."

## Phase 1 scope (build this)
- Image: auto-detect and remove the visible Gemini logo, on device. Batch up to 20.
- Video: upload a Veo/Gemini video (max 100 MB, max 60 s), server removes the logo,
  user downloads it. Files are deleted from the server right after.
- Before/after compare, save to gallery / download, share.
- Clear messages when no watermark is found or a file is too big.
- About page: privacy, what the app does not do (invisible SynthID stays), credits.

## Not in Phase 1
- Other AI tools' watermarks, manual "draw a box" removal, AI inpainting.
- Accounts, payments, history, cloud storage.
- The canvas / layer editor (Phase 2 of the business — separate plan).

## Success for v1
- A Gemini image is cleaned in under 2 s on a mid-range Android phone.
- A 8 s 720p Veo video is cleaned and returned in under 20 s.
- No visible logo left on all fixture files; non-Gemini files are untouched.
