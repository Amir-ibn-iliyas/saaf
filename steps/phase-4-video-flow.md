# Phase 4 — Video flow in the app

## 4.1 Upload with progress
- Upload with progress (XMLHttpRequest on web; `expo-file-system` upload task on native).
- Two-stage progress UI from the mockup; cancel aborts the request.
- Check size and duration on the device first and show errors before uploading.
**Done when:** upload progress moves smoothly; cancel works; server errors show DESIGN.md copy.

## 4.2 Video result and save
- Download result, preview with `expo-video`, save to gallery / download on web, share.
**Done when:** cleaned video plays in the gallery with sound on Android and downloads on web.
